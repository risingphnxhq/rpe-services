How we compare

Terms before headlines.

Our selection criteria are total cost, coverage fit, data conditions, device compatibility, portability and payment flexibility. An unknown condition stays “Not confirmed.” We do not claim hands-on testing unless we have performed and documented it.

Our referral relationship

Know who you’re buying from.

RPE Services is a comparison and referral destination. Once approved, eligible provider links may earn us a commission. We will identify those links clearly. The provider’s agreement governs service, billing, delivery and support.

Questions, answered plainly
Before you commit.
Can I order a service through RPE right now?
Ordering is not open yet. Supplier applications are submitted, and offers require acceptance plus confirmed equipment, pricing, coverage, support and payment terms before launch.
Does “unlimited” mean unlimited full-speed data?
Not necessarily. Each offer will identify the high-speed allowance, reduced speed, fair-use restrictions, and any service suspension threshold.
Can I move a plug-in home router?
The hardware may be portable while its service is restricted to a registered address. We will state whether moving it is permitted under the selected plan.
Who will charge me and support the equipment?
For the launch referral model, the supplier collects customer payments and handles equipment, activation, billing, cancellation, and network support under its agreement. RPE may receive eligible commissions after partner approval.
Can I use my existing router?
That requires confirmation of the model, supported network bands, SIM restrictions, and supplier compatibility. We will not promise compatibility before those checks.
How will RPE earn money?
Approved referral links may earn RPE commissions. The referral relationship will be disclosed beside the relevant offer. No commission tracking is active on this site yet.
What to check before choosing
No service price or availability is promised. These details must be confirmed before this category contains an orderable offer.
